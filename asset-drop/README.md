# asset-drop · incoming imagery for the overhaul

Drop raw images here (from ChatGPT image generation, Liliana's phone, or a
photographer) named by asset id from the overhaul board, e.g. `hero-runner.png`.
The imagery piece of the overhaul turns whatever lands here into responsive
webp + srcset in /public and wires it into the pages.

Policy (agreed 2026-08-30):
- People = real photos only. AI-generated humans on a medical site recreate
  the exact "AI-built" impression the overhaul exists to kill.
- AI generation (ChatGPT) is fine for: textures, abstract backgrounds,
  illustrations, iconography, OG-card art.
- Every AI prompt should include the anti-gloss guards: film grain, natural
  light, imperfect framing, muted color, no plastic skin, no HDR sheen.

This folder is gitignored; processed outputs land in /public.
