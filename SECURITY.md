# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Email `info@vivawellnessco.com` with the subject `Website security report` and include:

- the affected public URL or repository path;
- a concise description of the behavior and potential impact;
- reproducible steps that do not access, alter, or expose another person's data; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Do not include patient information, clinical messages, credentials, API keys, access tokens, or other secrets in the report. Do not submit live website forms as part of security testing.

Viva will acknowledge actionable reports when operationally possible. Please allow reasonable time for investigation and remediation before public disclosure.

## Scope and safe research

Good-faith review of the public website and this repository is welcome when it avoids:

- privacy violations or access to another person's data;
- denial of service, high-volume scanning, or cost amplification;
- social engineering, credential testing, or third-party account access;
- sending email, creating appointments, or submitting forms without authorization; and
- modifying or deleting production data.

Third-party services such as Cloudflare, Resend, GlossGenius, Plausible, and linked partner sites are governed by their own security programs and are not authorized targets through this policy.

## Supported version

Only the current production commit on `main` receives security fixes.
